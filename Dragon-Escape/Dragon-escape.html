You book it to the opening of the cave. running as fast as you can. the light is still far. The dragon stirs. You can see outside the tunnel now but just barely.

You hear the dragon get up. You hear your foot hit the ground in a loud echo. You hear a large footstep behind you. You pick up the pace.

The exit is in your sight, the dragon is catching up. However you manage to pull ahead.

After running for a while you come across a smoldering shelter and think there is no way the dragon will find you there.

When you enter inside you see two things that stand out to you. There is an Oak Staff with obsidian forged wrap around the curved end on the table sitting with a few books, and an elegant blacksteel sword that is impaled in a pedestal.
